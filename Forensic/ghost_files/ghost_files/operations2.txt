## GENERAL NOTES

modified without approval.

Old files should be preserved for audit purposes.

Do not remove files solely because they appear unused.

Before deleting anything from the archive, verify its ownership,
permissions, modification date, and backup status.

Some records may contain references to older systems that are no longer
active.

The recovery team should always use the latest manifest when restoring
the archive.
