# INCIDENT RESPONSE REPORT

Incident ID: IR-2047
Classification: INTERNAL
Status: CLOSED
Date: 2026-08-17

## SUMMARY

Anomalous activity was detected on the archive server during the early hours
of 17 August. Multiple authentication attempts were recorded against the
maintenance account.

The initial investigation did not identify evidence of data exfiltration.
The affected system was isolated while access records were reviewed.

## INVESTIGATION NOTES

The archive directory contained several historical reports and system
records. Investigators were unable to determine whether the directory
contents had been accessed before the maintenance session ended.

No confirmed evidence of credential theft was found.

The following files were reviewed:

system_backup.log
access_records.txt
operation_notes.txt
recovery_manifest.txt

No suspicious executable files were identified.

## RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Rotate maintenance credentials.
2. Review SSH access controls.
3. Disable unused accounts.
4. Audit hidden files within archived directories.
5. Preserve the original archive for further investigation.

## FINAL STATUS

The incident was classified as LOW severity.
